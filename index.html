Laboratory Services

We Test What We Sell — On Your Own Fluids

An in-house petroleum and water laboratory run by chemical engineers with more than a decade of oilfield laboratory experience. Analysis is performed to ASTM, APHA, API and NACE methods — the references your QA and inspection teams already work to.

45+ routine and special testsCrude, water, gas, amine, corrosion and drilling-fluid testing under one roof — no need to split samples between vendors.
Field sampling & on-site testingSampling at wellhead, separator, CPF and water plant with preserved, chain-of-custody samples; portable kits for time-critical parameters (DO, H₂S, residuals).
Reports that drive decisionsEvery report states the method, detection limit and trend against previous results, with a recommendation — not just a number.
Built for southern-Iraq conditionsHigh-salinity produced water above 200,000 mg/L TDS, H₂S-bearing streams and summer ambient temperatures are our normal operating envelope.
